A Note From the Founder

I'm Aneesa, a 29-year-old woman who spent years searching for pyjamas that were genuinely beautiful, made from materials I could trust, and designed for how I wanted to feel at home. I couldn't find them. So I decided to make them.

elowen eve was born from that search and from my belief that what touches your skin while you sleep matters. I designed every piece and print in the Australis in Bloom Collection, inspired by the flora of Australia, the country I call home. Each style is drawn with a modern vintage sensibility, romantic in detail and feminine in spirit, made from natural fibres free from toxic dyes and harmful chemicals.

Your home self matters, and it turns out, looking after your skin and wearing something truly beautiful aren't mutually exclusive. elowen eve is proof of that.
